動詞片語
=======

以下是英文學習者最常用、也最實用的 **動詞片語 (Phrasal Verbs)**，按照意思分類整理，並附上中文解釋與例句。這些幾乎涵蓋了日常對話、考試（TOEIC、IELTS、TOEFL）、職場英文的 90% 使用場景。

### 1. 起身／日常生活類
- get up 起床  
- wake up 醒來  
- go to bed 上床睡覺  
- fall asleep 睡著  
- get dressed 穿衣服  
- put on 穿上（衣服）  
- take off 脫掉（衣服／起飛）  
- turn on 打開（燈、電器）  
- turn off 關掉  
- pick up 撿起；接人；學會  
- hang up 掛斷電話；掛起來  
- throw away 扔掉  
- clean up 打掃乾淨

### 2. 外出／移動類
- go out 出去（玩樂）  
- come back 回來  
- get in 上車／進入  
- get off 下車  
- get on 上車  
- set off/out 出發  
- take off （飛機）起飛  
- run out of 用完、耗盡  
- drop by/off 順路拜訪／送人  
- check in 進來  
- go away 走開、離開

### 3. 溝通類
- call back 回電  
- hang up 掛電話  
- find out 查出、發現  
- look up 查（字典、網路）  
- speak up 說大聲點  
- fill in/out 填寫（表格）  
- point out 指出  
- talk over 詳細討論  
- get along with 與…相處融洽  
- catch up with 追上；敘舊

### 4. 工作／學習類
- turn in 繳交（作業）  
- hand in/out 交出／發放  
- carry on 繼續  
- put off 延後  
- look forward to 期待  
- give up 放棄  
- take up 開始從事（興趣）  
- work out 解決；運動；計算結果  
- figure out 想出、弄懂  
- go over 複習、檢查  
- catch up on 補完（落後的工作）

### 5. 關係／情感類
- break up 分手  
- make up 和好；化妝  
- get together 聚會  
- cheer up 讓…開心  
- calm down 冷靜下來  
- let down 讓…失望  
- turn down 拒絕  
- ask out 約出去  
- fall for 愛上  
- get over 克服；從（失戀、生病）恢復

### 6. 其他高頻片語
- run into 偶遇  
- come across 偶然發現  
- look after 照顧  
- take care of 照顧  
- pay back 還錢  
- pay off 還清（債務）；成功  
- show up 出現  
- end up 最後變成  
- grow up 長大  
- hold on 等一下（電話）  
- hurry up 快點  
- slow down 慢下來  
- come up with 想出（點子）  
- deal with 處理  
- rely on 依賴

### 1. 金錢相關
1. Cost an arm and a leg 極貴  
   → A new iPhone costs an arm and a leg.
2. Break the bank 花光積蓄  
   → I’d love a Ferrari, but it would break the bank.
3. Dirt cheap 非常便宜  
   → I got this jacket dirt cheap at the outlet.
4. Pay through the nose 被敲竹槓  
   → We paid through the nose for parking in London.
5. A piece of cake 輕而易舉 (= very easy)
6. Make ends meet 收支平衡  
   → With three kids, it’s hard to make ends meet.

### 2. 情緒與狀態
7. Over the moon 非常開心  
   → She was over the moon when she got accepted to Harvard.
8. Down in the dumps 心情低落  
   → He’s been down in the dumps since his breakup.
9. On cloud nine 極度幸福  
   → They’ve been on cloud nine since the wedding.
10. Hit the roof / Go through the roof 暴跳如雷  
    → My dad will hit the roof when he sees this bill.
11. Feeling under the weather 小病、不舒服  
    → I’m feeling under the weather, so I’ll stay home.
12. Kick the bucket 翹辮子（去世）  
    → He kicked the bucket at the age of 95.

### 3. 行動與決心
13. Bite the bullet 硬著頭皮做  
    → I hate the dentist, but I’ll just have to bite the bullet.
14. Burn the midnight oil 熬夜苦讀／加班  
    → I’ve been burning the midnight oil to finish this project.
15. Get cold feet 臨陣退縮  
    → He got cold feet and canceled the skydiving.
16. Go the extra mile 多付出努力  
    → She always goes the extra mile for her students.
17. Hit the ground running 一起步就全力衝刺  
    → Our new employee hit the ground running.

### 4. 成功與失敗
18. Hit the nail on the head 一針見血  
    → You hit the nail on the head—that’s exactly the problem.
19. Miss the boat 錯失良機  
    → I missed the boat on Bitcoin in 2010.
20. When pigs fly 不可能發生  
    → He’ll clean his room when pigs fly.
21. The ball is in your court 輪到你決定  
    → I’ve made an offer—now the ball is in your court.
22. Barking up the wrong tree 找錯對象  
    → If you think I stole it, you’re barking up the wrong tree.

### 5. 描述人與關係
23. A wolf in sheep’s clothing 笑裡藏刀的人  
24. The apple of one’s eye 掌上明珠  
25. Like two peas in a pod 形影不離、非常像  
26. Spill the beans 洩密、說漏嘴

27. Stab someone in the back 背後捅刀  
28. Get along like a house on fire 一見如故

### 6. 時間與速度

31. Time flies 光陰似箭  
32. Kill time 打發時間  
    → I played games to kill time at the airport.
33. Around the clock 全天 24 小時

### 51–100 常見英文 Idioms（續）

51. **Raining cats and dogs** 下大雨  
   → We can’t go out—it’s raining cats and dogs.

52. **A blessing in disguise** 因禍得福  
   → Losing that job was a blessing in disguise; now I have a better one.

53. **Beat a dead horse** 做白工、舊事重提  
   → Stop complaining about the past—you’re beating a dead horse.

54. **Bite off more than you can chew** 貪多嚼不爛  
   → Taking six courses this semester? You’ve bitten off more than you can chew.

55. **Can’t judge a book by its cover** 人不可貌相  
   → He looks scary, but he’s actually very kind—you can’t judge a book by its cover.

56. **Cross that bridge when we get to it** 船到橋頭自然直  
   → What if we don’t get funding? → We’ll cross that bridge when we get to it.

57. **Don’t count your chickens before they hatch** 別太早高興  
   → You didn’t get the job yet—don’t count your chickens before they hatch.

58. **Every cloud has a silver lining** 塞翁失馬焉知非福  
   → I didn’t get into my first-choice school, but every cloud has a silver lining.

59. **Get a taste of your own medicine** 以其人之道還治其人之身  
   → He always gossips, and now everyone’s gossiping about him—he got a taste of his own medicine.

60. **Give someone the benefit of the doubt** 先假設他是對的／暫時相信他  
   → I’m not sure if he forgot or lied, but I’ll give him the benefit of the doubt.

61. **Go down in flames** 慘敗、一敗塗地  
   → The presentation went down in flames when the PowerPoint crashed.

62. **In hot water** 有麻煩  
   → He’s in hot water with the boss after missing the deadline.

63. **Keep your chin up** 打起精神來  
   → I know you failed the exam, but keep your chin up!

64. **Let sleeping dogs lie** 別自找麻煩、別提舊事  
   → Don’t mention his ex-girlfriend—just let sleeping dogs lie.

65. **Make a long story short** 長話短說  
   → We got lost, argued, missed the flight… to make a long story short, we’re still here.

66. **Not playing with a full deck** 腦袋有問題  
   → He invested all his money in that scam? He’s not playing with a full deck.

67. **On thin ice** 如履薄冰、處境危險  
   → After being late three times, he’s on thin ice with the manager.

68. **Play it by ear** 隨機應變、見機行事  
   → We don’t have a plan for the weekend—let’s just play it by ear.

69. **Pull the plug** 停止、拔掉插頭（中止計劃）  
   → The project was too expensive, so they pulled the plug.

70. **Saved by the bell** 千鈞一髮被救  
   → I was about to get scolded when the meeting ended—saved by the bell!

71. **Shoot yourself in the foot** 自己害自己  
   → He shot himself in the foot by insulting the interviewer.

72. **Steal someone’s thunder** 搶某人的風頭  
   → I was going to announce my engagement, but my sister stole my thunder with her pregnancy news.

73. **Take it with a grain of salt** 半信半疑  
   → He said he’ll pay you back next week? Take it with a grain of salt.

74. **The elephant in the room** 大家心知肚明但不願提的問題  
   → His drinking problem is the elephant in the room.

75. **Throw in the towel** 認輸、放棄  
   → After 10 failed attempts, he threw in the towel.

76. **Under your nose** 就在眼前（卻沒看到）  
   → The keys were under your nose the whole time!

77. **Wear your heart on your sleeve** 情緒寫在臉上  
   → She wears his heart on his sleeve—you always know how he feels.

78. **You can’t have your cake and eat it too** 魚與熊掌不可兼得  
   → You want a high salary and short hours? You can’t have your cake and eat it too.

79. **A dime a dozen** 很普通、到處都是  
   → Freelance writers these days are a dime a dozen.

80. **Actions speak louder than words** 行動勝於空談  
   → He keeps saying he’ll help, but actions speak louder than words.

81. **Add fuel to the fire** 火上加油  
   → Don’t scold him now—you’ll just add fuel to the fire.

82. **Back to square one** 回到起點  
   → The negotiations failed, so we’re back to square one.

83. **Bark is worse than his bite** 刀子嘴豆腐心  
   → Don’t be scared of the boss—his bark is worse than his bite.

84. **Born with a silver spoon in one’s mouth** 生在富貴人家  
   → She was born with a silver spoon in her mouth and never had to work.

85. **Burn your bridges** 破壞關係、斷絕後路  
   → He quit by yelling at the boss—he really burned his bridges.

86. **By the skin of your teeth** 勉強成功、九死一生  
   → I passed the exam by the skin of my teeth.

87. **Don’t put all your eggs in one basket** 別孤注一擲  
   → Don’t invest all your money in crypto—don’t put all your eggs in one basket.

88. **Drive someone up the wall** 把某人逼瘋  
   → That loud music is driving me up the wall!

89. **Get your act together** 振作起來、收拾好自己  
   → If you want the promotion, get your act together!

90. **Hit the hay/sack** 去睡覺  
   → I’m exhausted—time to hit the hay.

91. **It’s not rocket science** 沒那麼難  
   → Making pasta is easy—it’s not rocket science.

92. **Jump the gun** 太早行動、搶跑  
   → He announced the product before it was ready—jumped the gun.

93. **Keep something at bay** 擋在外面、抑制  
   → Coffee helps me keep sleepiness at bay.

94. **Like riding a bicycle** 一學就會、永遠不忘  
   → I haven’t skated in years, but it’s like riding a bicycle.

95. **Make a mountain out of a molehill** 小題大做  
   → It’s just a tiny mistake—don’t make a mountain out of a molehill.

96. **No pain, no gain** 不勞無獲  
   → You want abs? No pain, no gain!

97. **Out of the woods** 脫離危險  
   → The company lost money but is now out of the woods.

98. **Put your foot in your mouth** 說錯話、失言  
   → I asked her when the baby is due… but she’s not pregnant. Totally put my foot in my mouth.

99. **Rome wasn’t built in a day** 羅馬不是一天造成的（成功需要時間）  
   → Learning English is hard, but Rome wasn’t built in a day.

100. **The early bird catches the worm** 早起的鳥兒有蟲吃  
    → I always arrive at 7 a.m.—the early bird catches the worm!
